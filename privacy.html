Privacy policy

NateData Analysis is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Nathanias YouTube channel.

What it reads

With the owner's permission it reads that one channel's own data through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API: the list of its videos and their statistics. Access is read-only. It cannot upload, edit, delete or post anything.

What it keeps and shares

The numbers are stored on the owner's own computer and are used only to review the channel's videos. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about any other person or channel is collected.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from his Google Account's third-party access page, which stops all reading at once.
